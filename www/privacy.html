Privacy Policy

OTI Recovery app · Over the Influence Recovery · Effective September 29, 2026

The OTI Recovery app is published by Over the Influence Recovery, a non-profit organization in San Diego, California. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

What the app collects

Most of the app works without any account and collects nothing: the calendar, help lines, tools, stories, and resources have no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting, and send no information about you to anyone.

The optional Community section is for OTI members only. If you join it, we store:

  • Your email address, used to send you a one-time sign-in code and to match you to OTI's member list.
  • The display name you choose and your member role (participant, coach, board member, admin).
  • The messages you post, any reports you file about a post, and which topics you have read.

This information is stored on OTI's private community server (hosted by Supabase in the United States) and is shown only to the member roles listed on each topic. For example, Daily Affirmations is visible only to participants and the coach. OTI staff who moderate a topic can hide or delete posts in it. We do not sell or share this information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

You can sign out at any time. To leave the Community or have your Community data deleted, email info@otirecovery.org; posts from a removed member are shown as "Former member" or deleted at your request.

Information stored only on your device

Two optional features keep information on your phone so the app can show it back to you:

  • Your first day (the sobriety day counter).
  • My people: names, phone numbers, and notes you add for your own call list.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up by the app, or shared. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also clear it inside the app at any time ("Stop counting" and the remove buttons in My people).

Notifications

If you turn on a meeting reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification service or server is involved and you can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Links to other services

The app links to third-party websites and services such as Zoom (support group), Donorbox (event tickets and donations), Thinkific (training courses), Google Calendar, and help lines. When you open a link, call, or text, that service's own privacy policy applies. The app does not track which links you open.

Optional content updates

To keep events and phone numbers current, the app may download an updated content file from Over the Influence Recovery's public web hosting when it opens. This request contains no personal information beyond the standard technical details of any web request (such as an IP address), which the hosting provider may log for security.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
